# Data preparation

This section records how the electricity demand and solar weather data were prepared before forecasting.

- [load_branch](load_branch/README.md): cleaning, filling missing readings, scaling and combining household demand.
- [solar_branch](solar_branch/README.md): filtering, filling gaps and averaging sunlight and temperature readings.

Open the PNG figures for a quick view, the TXT reports for explanations and the CSV tables for the underlying values. These are saved outputs; the original spreadsheets and preprocessing programs are not included. The prepared load series is available in [forecasting/data](../forecasting/data/README.md).
